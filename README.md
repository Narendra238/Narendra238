## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am an **Informatics and Data Science** student at **Universitas Ahmad Dahlan, Yogyakarta**, with a dedicated passion for **Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)** and **Operational Optimization**. I believe urban mobility bottlenecks and public transit inefficiencies are fundamentally data and algorithmic challenges waiting to be solved.

- 🎓 **Academic Base:** Informatics & Data Science @ Universitas Ahmad Dahlan, Yogyakarta.
- 🚦 **Core Interest:** Urban Mobility Systems, Public Transit Headway Optimization, Traffic Flow Modeling, and Geospatial Analytics.
- 🌱 **Current Exploration:** Predictive Machine Learning models, Graph Algorithms (`NetworkX`, Dijkstra/A*), and spatial analysis with `GeoPandas`.
- 💡 **Philosophy:** *"Transforming urban mobility challenges into clear, actionable, data-driven solutions."*
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Python programming, Data Cleaning & Analysis, Machine Learning, or Traffic & Transit Optimization.
